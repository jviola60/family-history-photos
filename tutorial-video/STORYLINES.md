# Storylines: Family History Photos Tutorial

Three distinct narrative approaches for the Family History Photos explainer tutorial video, following the HyperFrames story contract (`references/story.md`).

---

## Storyline 1 (Recommended): The Family Shoebox Journey

> **Why recommended:** The clearest explainer is a single worked example. A real user photo (Mom: Patricia Josephine Gill) travels from an unindexed scan in a folder to a restored, geocoded, FamilySearch-linked heirloom. The viewer understands both *what* to do and *why* it matters in under 45 seconds.

- **Device:** The Worked Example (`references/types/explainer.md` §2). One vintage scan enters the browser and travels through cataloging, map pinning, tree linking, AI restoration, and metadata baking.
- **Form:** Object-to-object chain (`references/story.md` §2). Every shot lands on the exact UI element that triggers the next step (Folder Drop → Map Pin → Tree PID Dropdown → 4K Face Restore Slider → 1-Click Save to File).
- **First Two Seconds:** Frame 0 opens directly on an unindexed, slightly faded family photo dropping into the browser workspace with the title: *"Turn lost shoebox scans into permanent family history."*
- **Beats:** 
  `Hook (Folder drop & 100% private in-browser index) → Pin Location (Interactive Leaflet map & geocoding) → Tag & Sync PID (Live FamilySearch tree search: BM6T-WQ5) → Restore in Photo Lab (4K Face Restore curtain wipe) → 1-Click Save to File (EXIF/XMP metadata baked + FamilySearch Memories synced)`
- **Look:** Archival Studio. Obsidian ground (`#0b0f17`), slate surface (`#1e293b`), crisp typography with Inter & Playfair Display, photo accents in Kodak amber (`#f59e0b`) and verified emerald (`#10b981`).
- **Sound:** Warm acoustic guitar progression with physical tactile UI clicks, camera shutter foley, and pin drop chime.

---

## Storyline 2: The Genealogist's Light Table

- **Device:** The Object is the Explanation. The web application is framed as a modern digital light table where spatial memory (where did they live?) meets generative AI restoration.
- **Form:** Flight Through One World. The camera moves spatially across three dedicated macro-views: The Global Map (interactive geographical pins across decades), The Ledger Grid (bulk tagging & relative relationships), and The Restoration Darkroom (the 6 AI repair engines).
- **First Two Seconds:** Camera glides over a glowing world map populated with pinned vintage photos, zooming in tight to Chicago and Bowling Green pins.
- **Beats:**
  `Spatial Hook (Global pin light table) → Ledger Inspection (Relatives, decades, relationships) → Photo Lab Deep Dive (Face repair, colorization, tone balance) → The Dual Export (Local file preservation + Cloud FamilySearch upload)`
- **Look:** Dark cartographic navy (`#080c14`), parchment lines (`#cbd5e1`), topographic contour accents (`#38bdf8`).
- **Sound:** Ambient synth pad with rhythmic metronome tick and subtle whooshes between spatial zones.

---

## Storyline 3: The 60-Second Quickstart

- **Device:** Feature as the Hook / Kinetic UI. Fast-paced, punchy, high-tempo product demonstration designed for first-time testers and quick onboarding.
- **Form:** Cut on the beat with punch-in magnifiers. Split screens, crisp animated cursors, and instant visual before-and-after proof.
- **First Two Seconds:** A split-screen before/after of a blurry 1968 photo snapping into crystal-clear 4K detail in 0.5s with a camera snap sound.
- **Beats:**
  `Instant Proof (4K Face Restore before/after) → Step 1: Open Local Folder (No cloud upload needed, private) → Step 2: Tag & FamilySearch PID auto-complete → Step 3: Map Pinning → Step 4: One-Click Save (Embedded EXIF preservation)`
- **Look:** Clean high-contrast minimal dark theme (`#0d1117`), electric cobalt accent (`#2563eb`), bold grotesque sans typography.
- **Sound:** Upbeat modern lo-fi beat with crisp snap on cuts.

---

## Decision
Proceeding with **Storyline 1 (The Family Shoebox Journey)** as the master tutorial specification in `SPEC.md`.
