Privacy Policy — Family History Photos

Effective Date: November 2026

Family History Photos is a client-side web application hosted at https://jviola60.github.io/family-history-photos/ that helps users organize, restore, and preserve family history photographs.

1. Data We Access & How It Is Used

  • Google Drive & Google Photos: When you choose to connect Google Drive or Google Photos, the app requests OAuth 2.0 access tokens directly in your browser to list folders, verify file MD5 checksums, import selected family photos, or upload missing local backup files to your own Google Drive.
  • FamilySearch: When you sign in to FamilySearch, the app uses OAuth 2.0 tokens directly in your browser to read/update your selected ancestor records and upload photo memories you explicitly choose.
  • Local Processing Only: Family History Photos has no backend database that stores your personal photos, OAuth tokens, or Google Drive contents. All scanning, checksum comparisons, GEDCOM parsing, and session state stay in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB).

2. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with any third parties or advertisers. Photos and metadata are only transmitted directly between your browser and the services you explicitly authorize (Google Drive, Google Photos, FamilySearch, or optional user-configured AI restoration endpoints).

3. Revoking Access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account Permissions page, or clear local browser data in your browser settings.

4. Contact

For questions or support, please open an issue on the Family History Photos GitHub repository.
